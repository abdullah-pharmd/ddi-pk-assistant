# Paper Title Options

Calibrated against `research_novelty_assessment.md` — none claim more than the
project's actual scope (4 drugs, 6 DDI pairs, prototype-stage validation) supports.

1. "An Integrated, Explainable Decision-Support Prototype for Pharmacokinetic
   Dosing, Renal Adjustment, and Drug-Drug Interaction Risk: A Feasibility Study"

2. "Cross-Domain Integration of Pharmacokinetics, Renal Function, and Drug
   Interaction Risk in a Small-Scale Clinical Decision-Support Tool"

3. "Development of a Rule-Based and Explainable Machine-Learning Hybrid System for
   Patient-Specific Dosing Support: A Four-Drug Case Study"

4. "Toward Patient-Specific Dosing Support: Integrating Renal Function, Drug
   Interactions, and Pharmacokinetics in a Fully-Sourced Prototype Tool"

## Titles explicitly avoided, and why
- Anything with "novel AI system" or "predictive model" — the ML component is an
  audit tool, not a validated predictor (Stage 11/12)
- Anything implying clinical readiness ("clinical decision support system" without
  qualifiers like "prototype" or "feasibility") — contradicts Stage 1's core
  requirement
- Anything overstating drug/DDI coverage ("comprehensive")  — the scope is
  deliberately 4 drugs / 6 pairs

## Recommendation
Option 1 or 2 — both state the actual contribution (integration) without
overclaiming, and "feasibility study" / "small-scale" honestly signal the project's
actual stage to a reviewer before they even open the paper.
