Source book (all 3 equations below): **Bauer LA. *Applied Clinical Pharmacokinetics*, 2nd ed. New York: McGraw-Hill; 2008.**

| Phenytoin | `Rate = (Vmax × C) / (Km + C)` | — | — | **Richens A, Dunlop A. Serum phenytoin levels in the management of epilepsy: a scheme for dose increments. *Lancet*. 1975;2(7928):247-248.** (journal article — no textbook page needed) |

No formal equation numbers are assigned to any of these in the Bauer textbook.

**Note on gentamicin:** unlike vancomycin/digoxin, Bauer Ch. 4 has no equation regressing Cl directly on CrCl. Instead, renal function first estimates the elimination rate constant:

`ke = 0.00293(CrCl) + 0.014` (ke in h⁻¹, CrCl in mL/min; 0.014 = nonrenal ke in anephric patients) — p. 110, graphed in Fig. 4-2 (p. 105).

Clearance is then derived as `Cl = ke × Vd` (Table 4-2B, p. 114).
